# Module 05 — Training & Communication Testing

## Objective

This module simulates data-driven testing over AML lifecycle training, communication and customer knowledge culture.

The objective is to identify areas, geographies or teams where AML lifecycle standards have not been properly communicated, acknowledged or completed through training.

---

## Why This Matters

AML Lifecycle Transformation is not only about defining policies, standards or controls.

Those standards must be understood and applied by the areas involved in the customer lifecycle, including Business, Compliance, Risk, Engineering and Operations.

If training and communication are weak, the institution may have strong AML standards on paper but inconsistent execution in practice.

---

## Tests Performed

### 1. Critical areas without AML lifecycle training completed

Identifies critical areas where AML lifecycle training has not been completed.

This may indicate weaknesses in AML culture, awareness or readiness to apply corporate standards.

---

### 2. Training deadline missed

Identifies training initiatives where the deadline has passed but completion has not been achieved.

This may indicate weak follow-up, insufficient prioritisation or poor governance tracking.

---

### 3. Low completion rate in AML lifecycle training

Identifies training initiatives with a completion rate below 80%.

This may indicate poor adoption, lack of management attention or insufficient communication.

---

### 4. Communication sent but not acknowledged by target area

Identifies AML communications that have been sent but not acknowledged by the relevant business area.

This may indicate a gap between communication and actual receipt or understanding.

---

### 5. Acknowledgement deadline missed

Identifies communications where the acknowledgement deadline has passed.

This may indicate weak communication governance or insufficient ownership.

---

### 6. Areas involved in onboarding without updated KYC training

Identifies areas involved in customer onboarding where KYC training has not been completed or updated within the expected period.

This may indicate that teams involved in customer knowledge processes are not sufficiently trained.

---

## Governance Interpretation

Training and communication are key mechanisms for embedding AML lifecycle transformation.

A corporate AML standard only creates value if the relevant teams understand it, acknowledge it and apply it consistently.

From an AML Holding perspective, training and communication testing helps assess whether the transformation has moved from policy design to organizational adoption.

---

## Potential Findings

Potential governance findings may include:

* critical areas without completed AML training;
* overdue training actions;
* low completion rates in relevant geographies;
* communications sent but not acknowledged;
* onboarding teams without updated KYC training;
* weak evidence of AML lifecycle culture adoption.

---

## Recommended Management Actions

Recommended actions may include:

1. Define mandatory AML lifecycle training for critical areas.
2. Track completion rates by geography, business area and target population.
3. Escalate overdue training and acknowledgement gaps.
4. Link onboarding-related roles to mandatory KYC training.
5. Monitor communication acknowledgement through governance reporting.
6. Perform periodic effectiveness reviews of AML training and communication.

---

## Interview Explanation

I included this module because the AML Lifecycle Transformation role refers to leading training, communication and customer knowledge culture initiatives to mitigate AML/CFT risk.

The purpose of this module is to show how SQL can be used to test whether AML lifecycle standards have been properly communicated, acknowledged and completed through training across relevant areas and geographies.

The objective is not to design a training programme, but to demonstrate how data analytics can support AML Governance by identifying adoption gaps, overdue actions and weaknesses in AML culture deployment.
