# Module 06 — Critical Reporting Testing

## Objective

This module simulates data-driven testing over critical AML lifecycle reporting.

The objective is to consolidate exceptions from different AML lifecycle areas into governance indicators that can support AML Holding, Compliance and senior management reporting.

---

## Why This Matters

AML Lifecycle Transformation requires reliable reporting.

A global AML function needs to understand where the main lifecycle weaknesses are concentrated: customer risk rating, documentation, restrictive measures, geography rollout, training, communication and critical report submission.

Without structured reporting, AML issues may remain fragmented across teams, geographies or processes.

Critical reporting helps transform operational exceptions into governance visibility.

---

## Tests Performed

### 1. Customer risk rating inconsistencies by country

Aggregates potential customer risk rating inconsistencies by country.

This helps identify geographies where the customer risk rating framework may require review or quality assurance.

---

### 2. Documentation gaps by AML rating

Aggregates documentation gaps by assigned AML rating.

This helps identify whether higher-risk customers have documentation weaknesses that require remediation.

---

### 3. Restrictive measure gaps by country

Aggregates restrictive measure gaps by country.

This helps identify where high-risk or critical customers may not have appropriate restrictive or enhanced measures applied.

---

### 4. Geography rollout gaps by region

Aggregates AML lifecycle rollout gaps by region.

This helps identify where corporate standards may not have been fully implemented or remediated.

---

### 5. Training and communication gaps by business area

Aggregates training, completion and acknowledgement gaps by business area.

This helps identify areas where AML lifecycle culture, awareness or adoption may require reinforcement.

---

### 6. Critical reports overdue or not submitted

Identifies critical AML reports that have not been submitted after their due date.

This may indicate weaknesses in reporting discipline, ownership or escalation.

---

### 7. Critical report items open without escalation

Identifies critical reports with high-severity issues that have not been escalated.

This may indicate insufficient governance attention over material AML lifecycle issues.

---

### 8. Executive AML lifecycle KRI summary

Consolidates exceptions across the main AML lifecycle areas into a single executive KRI summary.

The purpose is to support prioritisation and governance discussion.

---

## Governance Interpretation

Critical reporting is the bridge between operational AML issues and senior-level governance.

AML Holding needs visibility over:

* where customer risk rating inconsistencies are concentrated;
* where documentation gaps remain open;
* where restrictive measures are not aligned with risk;
* where rollout implementation is delayed;
* where training and communication have not been completed;
* where critical reports are overdue or not escalated.

The purpose of reporting is not only to describe issues, but to support prioritisation, ownership, escalation and remediation.

---

## Potential Findings

Potential governance findings may include:

* customer risk rating inconsistencies concentrated in specific countries;
* documentation gaps affecting higher-risk customers;
* restrictive measure gaps in specific geographies;
* rollout gaps by region;
* low training or communication adoption in key business areas;
* overdue critical AML reports;
* high-severity issues not escalated;
* fragmented AML lifecycle visibility.

---

## Recommended Management Actions

Recommended actions may include:

1. Define standard AML lifecycle KRIs for Holding-level reporting.
2. Consolidate customer lifecycle exceptions into a single governance dashboard.
3. Track issues by geography, business area, customer risk rating and severity.
4. Escalate overdue reports and high-severity issues.
5. Assign clear ownership for remediation actions.
6. Monitor recurring gaps and trends over time.
7. Use reporting outputs to prioritise remediation and training efforts.

---

## Interview Explanation

I included this module because the AML Lifecycle Transformation role refers to participating actively in the preparation of critical reports.

The purpose of this module is to show how SQL can consolidate exceptions across customer risk rating, documentation standards, restrictive measures, rollout, training and reporting into governance indicators.

The objective is not to create a real regulatory report, but to demonstrate how data analytics can support AML Holding by transforming fragmented lifecycle issues into structured reporting, prioritisation and escalation.
